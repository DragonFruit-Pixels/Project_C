## OVERVIEW

At the start of each game, players choose a Vault box and a Sleeper box and combine their components. The Vault will provide the map layout, the working they are trying to disrupt (and how to disrupt it), as well as the abilities of the brood in the game. The Sleeper will provide the big boss they will fight, with its own challenges, along with its special minions. **Do not** combine elements from other boxes.

1 to 5 players control sworn, cooperating to win or lose the game together. In each game, the goal remains the same: disrupt the working, then kill the Sleeper.

## VAULT BOARD

The Vault board, used in every game, is the hub of information.

The Waking track shows the progression of the Sleeper as it manifests into our world. The Sleeper figure starts on the left part of the track and progresses to the right. When it enters a red space, it will be summoned (it will be summoned earlier if the working is disrupted). After the Sleeper is summoned to our world, the Rousing token is placed on the track and moved instead of the figure. If the Rousing token reaches the end of the track, the Sleeper dominates the world and the sworn lose!

## HOARD CARDS

Each vault has 15 Hoard cards. These include Items, Hirelings, and Curses that can help or hinder the players. Most Hoard cards can be claimed by fulfilling the requirements listed in the card’s central section. Sworn claim either the left or right side of the card, tucking them under their sworn board on the appropriate side. Hoard cards are further explained on page 16.

If it’s not specified when the player may use an effect on a Hoard card, it may be used anytime during their turn. However, it cannot be used while resolving another card, effect, or during a roll.

## PORTENT CARDS

Portent cards drive the horrors of each game. Players will grow to hate them. Half the Portent cards in a game come from the Vault box and the other half comes from the Sleeper box.

Each turn, after taking their actions, the active player will draw a Portent card. Some will move and/or summon foes. Others will drive sworn to bloom. There are also ones that aren’t as kind. They are explained in detail on page 15.

[SUMMONING] Most Portent cards have a Waking symbol on them. When there are 3 Waking symbols in the Portent discard pile, the Sleeper advances along the Waking track (see page 18).

## FOES

The term “foes” refers to Binders, brood, and the Sleeper (once it’s summoned to the board). Any foe that’s not a Binder or the Sleeper is a “brood”. Anything that refers to Binders affects Binders only. Anything that refers to brood affects brood only. Anything that refers to the Sleeper affects the Sleeper only.

Foe cards show their health, the dice they use when attacking, and a special ability that may come into effect in different circumstances. The cards also show the total number of figures that should be available in the pool. If a Vault uses specific brood, all its miniatures that are not present in the setup are kept in a pool, ready to spawn.

## SWORN

Each sworn has a board with a wound track, a sap track, a marrow track, and 3 skills. All are tracked with plastic blight markers in the sworn’s color. There are also slots on the right and on the left of the board to insert claimed Hoard cards. There is no limit to the number of cards that can be slotted to a sworn.

### SAP

Sap is used to try and push situations in the sworn’s favor. Players may always increase sworn’s sap to reroll 1 die they just rolled. Many Hoard cards also have sworn take sap in order to claim them. Generally, the only consequence of being at maximum sap is that the sworn can’t take more sap (though some effects may deal wounds instead). Sworn can take a maximum of 4 sap without healing.

### WOUNDS

If the marker ever reaches the skull at the end of the wound track, the sworn is dead (see Death of a Sworn on page 19).

### SKILLS

The sworn’s 3 skills allow them to bend the rules in their favor. The top one is their signature skill, unique to each sworn, while the other 2 skills come from a common pool (so other sworn may have the same ones). All skills start at the first level, but sworn will increase them when they level up. Some skills replace a lower-level skill as they level up, while others add to them, as described on the skill. The description of each of the common skills can be found on page 22.

### MARROW

Whenever a sworn loses marrow, the marker on their marrow track advances to the right. If it reaches the skull at the end, the sworn is lost to the brood and eliminated (see Ending the Game on page 20).

The track has Bloom ([INSANITY-THRESHOLD]) spaces that will activate the sworn’s bloom and level up a skill. Some of them also give sworn permanent boon dice. When a sworn loses more marrow (from dice rolls, Hoard, or Portent cards) than required to hit the next [INSANITY-THRESHOLD], the marker stops at the [INSANITY-THRESHOLD] and all excess marrow loss is ignored.

#### - Gaining Boon Dice

4 of the [INSANITY-THRESHOLD] spaces display a green Boon die [BONUS-DIE] under them. Once each of these is reached, the sworn adds +1 Boon die to any rolls they make for the rest of the  game, except for rolls against themselves (see page 11).

#### - Activating Blooms

Each sworn gets a bloom card at the start of play. Every time their marker on the marrow track reaches a [INSANITY-THRESHOLD], that bloom activates. Perform the symptoms on the card. If multiple sworn reach a [INSANITY-THRESHOLD] at the same time, they activate clockwise from the current player.

#### - Leveling up Skills

When the marker reaches a [INSANITY-THRESHOLD], after activating their bloom, the sworn levels up 1 of their skills. Choose 1 skill and move its marker 1 space to the right

## DICE AND CHECKS

Throughout the game, players roll dice to determine the outcome of certain actions or effects. The most common times to roll dice are when players are told to make a roll and when a foe attacks.

### Make a Roll

Some actions and Hoard cards tell players to "make a roll". When players make a roll, they always roll 3 standard black dice (and may also be allowed to add boon green dice).

### Foe Attack

Foes roll a specific number and type of dice for their attacks as indicated on their reference card.

### Rolls Against Yourself

Some effects and abilities specify "rolls against yourself". Rolls against a player are rolls where each success is detrimental to the player. Foe attacks and rolling for Fire (see page 17) are examples of "rolls against yourself".

Each die has 4 different possible results:

- [SUCCESS] **Success:** This means that the sworn (or the foe) succeeded at their attempt (or partially succeeded). If the sworn was attacking, it means they hit. If a foe is attacking, it means they hit. In some cases, players need to reach a target amount of successes in a single roll.

- [ELDER-SIGN] **Glyph**: These mean nothing unless a sworn has a skill or card that uses them.

- [TENTACLE] **Blight:** The Brood! For each blight, the sworn loses 1 marrow, moving their tracker 1 space to the right.

> **IMPORTANT:** Blights on EVERY roll cost sworn marrow, whether they're attacking, being attacked, or just "making a roll".

- [BLANK] **Blank:** No effect most of the time.

### BOON DICE

Certain skills and cards may give sworn boon green dice, as noted on their description. Some checks may also give them boon dice if they have a related ability. Finally, reaching certain Blooms will add permanent boon dice to all of that sworn's rolls, except against themself.

> **NOTE:** The boon dice do not contain a blight icon.

> **NOTE:** There is no limit to the number of regular dice and boon dice that may be added to a roll (if players run out of dice, note the results and roll the same dice again).

> **NOTE:** When rolling dice, players cannot choose to roll less dice than they are allowed to roll (unless the effect is optional).

### REROLLS

After rolling dice, players may take 1 sap to reroll 1 die, ignoring its original result. Players may do this as often as they like on any roll they make, until the sworn's sap is at the maximum. When foes attack, players roll for them and may also take sap to reroll their dice.

### MODIFYING ROLLS

Some effects or abilities may be applied to rolls. Unless stated otherwise, those abilities are applied to ALL rolls a sworn makes, including against themselves. Note, though, that some of them are optional.

### APPLYING RESULTS

Once a player is done using any rerolls, tally the number of each result obtained (note that some die faces contain 2 results). Make sure to apply any symbol changes from abilities (such as counting [ELDER-SIGN] as successes).

> **NOTE:** If an effect counts an icon as another icon (e.g. Glyphwork), that icon stops counting as the original icon, unless stated otherwise in the effect. That means that an icon cannot be changed by 2 different abilities, unless one of those abilities says the icon keeps counting as the original icon.

Then, apply the results of the dice roll in this order:

#### 1. Use Any Successes:

- If a sworn is attacking, these successes are wounds applied to their target.
- If a foe is attacking, successes are also wounds applied to their target (usually a sworn).
- If players are told to "make a roll" by an action or effect, they must follow the instructions on the corresponding action or effect. Sometimes, they must match or exceed the number of successes indicated on the card to be successful. In other cases, for each success rolled, players get to do something.

> **[Figure]** This vault allows the sworn to make a special action. While they are in a space without Binders, make a roll. Then, they move the nearest Binder 1 space towards them for each success rolled.

#### 2. Resolve Effects:

- Resolve any effects that may be tied to the result of the roll.

*(Examples: "If wounded...", "If it survives an attack...", "When the foe dies...")*

#### 3. Apply Any Blights:

- Lose 1 marrow for each blight rolled (move the marker 1 space to the right on their marrow track).

- If the sworn reaches a [INSANITY-THRESHOLD] space on their marrow track, stop moving the marker (even if they didn't lose the full amount of marrow), activate their bloom, and level up a skill (see Marrow on page 8).

## 1. TAKE 3 ACTIONS

On a player's turn, they may take 3 actions. They may take the same action more than once. There are 4 actions that are available in every vault and 2 that are unique to each vault. Also, some effects may give players access to free and special actions.

Some actions can only be done while in a Warded space. A Warded space is a space without foes.

### FREE ACTIONS

Some abilities and effects can be used as a free action. They can only be used during this Phase of the turn before or after other actions.

### SPECIAL ACTIONS

Some abilities and effects can be used as a special action. They can only be used during this Phase of the turn and players must spend 1 of their actions to use it.

### RUN

Move up to 3 spaces. Sworn can only move between spaces if there is a passage on both tiles (as indicated by arrows on both sides). They may also pass between 2 spaces that both have a staircase or tunnel token.

When sworn leave a space with foes, ALL foes in that space follow them to their new space (even if there were other sworn in their space). When sworn leave a space with Fire tokens, place 1 Fire token from the reserve onto their sworn board for each token on the space, leaving those tokens untouched (see Fire! on page17).

> **NOTE:** *Unless stated otherwise, when a sworn moves outside of a Run action, they catch fire and foes follow as well, even if it's not their turn. The only exception are the effects that "place" a sworn in another space, in which case the sworn is placed directly in that space, they do not catch fire, and foes do not follow.*

> **[Example]** *EXAMPLE: 1- Corvin uses a Run action to move up to 3 spaces. He first moves into a space with a Binder that has a wound.*

> **[Example]** *2- He keeps moving and the Binder follows him to the new space. The second space has 2 Fire tokens, but no foes.*

> **[Example]** *3- Corvin finishes his Run by moving into a third space, which contains a Harpy. Again, the Binder follows him. He is also leaving a space with 2 Fire tokens, so he takes 2 Fire tokens from the reserve and adds them to his sworn board. He is now in a space with a Harpy and the Binder who followed him. He is also on Fire. Pretty typical evening.*

### ATTACK

Target a single foe in the sworn's space and make a roll (see page 11). Each success rolled causes 1 wound to the target. Place wound tokens next to the base of the figure attacked. If this number equals (or exceeds) their health, they die. Remove their figure from the board. Keep in mind that if the Sleeper is on the board, it can only be attacked once the working is disrupted (see page 19).

> **[Example]** *EXAMPLE: 1- Corvin uses an action to attack a Binder and a Harpy. Normally, he would have to target either the Binder or the Harpy, but his level 2 Brawling skill enables him to target all foes in his space, so he doesn't have to choose.*

> **[Example]** *2- He takes 3 standard dice (players always take 3 standard dice when making a roll) and 1 boon die for his Brawling skill.*

> **[Example]** *3- He rolls and gets a glyph, a blank, a success + blight, and blight. Corvin doesn't have a skill that utilizes the glyph, so it has no effect. Corvin takes a sap to reroll the glyph, since it would make him lose a sap anyway because of the Harpy's special ability and gets a blank. Corvin's signature skill, Proficient, allows him to reroll an additional die. He could reroll the same die again, but chooses to reroll the blank result on the standard die and gets a success! He decides to stop rerolling.*

> **[Example]** *4- The Binder can take 2 wounds, and has already taken 1, and the Harpy can take 3. Corvin has 2 successes to distribute as hits. He decides to use 1 to kill the Binder, removing the figure and, using his Brawling skill, applies the last success as a hit to the Harpy.*

> **NOTE:** *If the sworn didn't have the Brawling skill, he would have to deal all wounds to a single foe (chosen before rolling dice), even if excess wounds would go ignored with no effect.*

> **[Example]** *5- Finally, Corvin loses 2 Marrow from the blights rolled. 2 steps closer to the brood.*

### BIND (only on a Warded space)

If a sworn is in a Warded space (there are no foes with them) they may heal their sap and/or health up to 3 spaces (in any combination). This is 3 spaces total, not 3 spaces for each track. Sworn may perform the Bind action more than once per turn.

> **IMPORTANT:** **MARROW CANNOT BE HEALED BY BINDING!**

> **[Example]** *EXAMPLE: Bertrude is at full sap and has taken 1 wound. She spends an action to Bind. She could choose to heal 1 wound and 2 sap, but decides to heal 3 sap instead, leaving her health where it is.*

### TRADE

The active sworn and all sworn in their space may trade any number of Items or Hirelings with each other (either giving or receiving). While some Hoard cards have Items or Hirelings on both the left and right side of the card, a traded card must remain on the same side as it was originally. **Sworn may not trade Curses**.

## 2. DRAW PORTENT CARD

Draw the top Portent card from the deck and resolve it. Then, place it on a faceup discard pile.

Portent cards may have several different steps to them, divided into 3 types. Players must do these steps in order, from top to bottom, before discarding the card. If players can't do a step on the card, skip it and move on to the next.

### WAKING

The Waking symbol has no effect when the card is drawn, but may cause the Sleeper to advance on its track at the end of the turn (see page 18). If the card has this symbol, make sure it remains visible when it is discarded.

### SPECIAL EFFECTS

Portent cards have all sorts of different special effects to make life hell. Unless otherwise stated, these effects only affect the player whose turn it is, ignoring all other sworn. Any choice that needs to be made is up to the active player, such as choosing which closest figure to move.

When an effect moves a foe towards a space, it should be moved by the shortest path to that space. Count how many spaces the foe would move in all possible paths and choose the shortest one. In case of a tie, the active player chooses. The foe must always be moved as much as allowed towards its target but stops when it reaches its target.

### SUMMON FOES

## DEATH OF A SWORN

When a sworn is killed or lost to the brood, the game is immediately lost if the Sleeper is still on the Waking track. If, however, the Sleeper has already been summoned to the board, the other sworn may continue playing and trying to win the game. First, discard all Hoard cards, tokens and other components the now-deceased sworn had. The vault may specify something to do with any special tokens the sworn had been holding.

If the sworn died during their turn, skip all the following turn phases except for the Check the Portent Discard Pile phase. If there are 3 Waking symbols, the Sleeper advances and its effects are applied (if it is needed to determine the position of the dead sworn, use the space where they died). From now on, that player's turn is skipped entirely.

## DISRUPTING THE WORKING

Each vault describes the tasks the sworn must accomplish in order to disrupt the Binders' working and make the Sleeper mortal, even if only for a short time. The Sleeper cannot be attacked or damaged until the working is disrupted (though it can attack sworn if it's on the board)!

If the sworn disrupt the working, the Sleeper is summoned at the end of the turn (see Check the Waking on the previous page).

## FIGHTING THE SLEEPER

Once the Sleeper is summoned to the board, it acts like any foe, attacking sworn that end their turn in its space. The number and type of dice the Sleeper rolls is the total of all dice displayed on all its revealed Stage cards.

Until the working is disrupted, the Sleeper cannot be attacked or damaged, even if it's already been summoned to the board by reaching the red space on the waking track! After the working is disrupted, the Sleeper can be attacked and damaged like any other foe. The Sleeper has multiple Stages, with individual health totals, which must be defeated one at a time until it's ultimately destroyed.

**Starting with Stage 2, each card displays**:
- A one-time effect that takes place when that card is revealed.
- An ongoing effect that remains in play until the end of the game.
- Health showing how many wounds that Stage can take.
- The number and type of dice that Stage adds to the Sleeper's attacks.

When a Stage has wounds equal to or greater than its health, that Stage is defeated. Move that Stage card to the side to reveal the next and resolve its When Revealed effect. Any excess wounds do NOT carry over to the next Stage. Remember that all of the previous Stages' effects and dice remain in play until the end of the game!

> **[Example]** *EXAMPLE: A sworn attacks KHARZUL while it is in Stage 2. That Stage already has 9 wounds from earlier attacks. The attacking sworn does 5 wounds! This new total of 14 is enough to defeat this Stage's 12 health. The Stage card is moved to the side, next to Stage 1, revealing Stage 3. Even though the sworn only needed 12 wounds to defeat the Stage and did 14, the extra 2 wounds do NOT carry over to Stage 3. KHARZUL is moved to the Yellow Rift and each sworn gains 1 Cinder token unless they take 2 sap. KHARZUL now rolls 3 boon dice AND 2 standard dice when attacking!*

## COMPONENT LIMITATION

With the exception of dice and Wound tokens (and consequently, Fire on sworn), all components are limited. If there are not enough tokens (or other components), use as many as possible for the effect, but do not add substitutes for the missing components. Also, if a component is discarded/removed, it returns to the pool of available components, unless stated otherwise.

When mixing components from different *The Waking* Core Boxes, players should increase the Fire token pool. For example, since *The Waking Core Box* comes with 8 Fire tokens and *The Waking - The Sunken* Core Box with 12, if players have both boxes, they increase the pool to 20 Fire tokens.

Note that this is not true for other components. For example, the total number of Binders in a Vault is always limited to 10, as stated in the Sleeper Minions Card.

## RESOLVING TIES AND AMBIGUITIES

If there are multiple ways to resolve an effect, the active player always chooses how to resolve it. For example, if an effect moves the nearest foe and there are multiple foes at the same distance, the active player chooses 1 of them to move. Also, if multiple effects trigger at the same time, the active player chooses the order in which they are resolved.

For several effects, players must determine the nearest or furthest element (space, figure, or token) from their sworn or another element on the board. When determining the nearest or furthest element, always use the shortest path to the space where the element is. Distance to the same space is always 0. So, if an element is in the sworn's space, it is the nearest element. If there are multiple elements of the specified type at the same distance, the active player selects 1 of them.

## CHECKING DISTANCE

For several effects players must determine the nearest or furthest element (space, figure or token) from their sworn or another element on the board. When determining the nearest or furthest element, always use the shortest path to the space where the element is. Distance to the same space is always 0. So, if an element is in the sworn's space, it is the nearest element. If there are multiple elements of the specified type at the same distance, the active player selects 1 of them.

Effects that block passages, like locked tokens, should be considered when checking the shortest path, the path cannot pass through blocked passages. The only exception is if the effect would move a figure that can ignore the restrictions. For example, on Season 3 - Vault 1: Isle of the Damned, the Binders can pass through locked tokens. If an effect moves the nearest Binder towards you, even though other figures cannot move through the locked tokens, you ignore those tokens when checking which Binder is the nearest, since they can pass through them. However, effects that affect which spaces are adjacent to each other on specific circumstances, like the Silver Key Talisman, are ignored when checking the shortest path.

## ENDING THE GAME

**Players win the game** when they kill the Sleeper by defeating its Final Stage. If multiple end-game triggers happen at the same time, winning conditions have precedence.

> **[Example]** *EXAMPLE: The Sleeper and the last sworn die at the same time. The players saved the world and win the game.*

**Players lose the game** when 1 of the following happens:
- A sworn is killed or lost to the brood before the Sleeper is summoned to the board (Stage 1 card).
- All sworn are killed or lost to the brood after the Sleeper is summoned (Stage 2 onward).
- The Rousing token reaches the last (8th) space of the Waking track.

## RULES SUMMARY

### TURN SEQUENCE

#### 1. SWORN TAKES 3 ACTIONS

- **Run** - Move up to 3 spaces - Foes follow. Add 1 [FIRE] to the sworn board per [FIRE] in the space they leave.
- **Attack** - Make a roll against 1 foe in sworn’s space
- **Bind** - Warded space only: Heal 3 sap/wounds..
- **Trade** - With all sworn in same space.
- **Vault actions** - Described on the vault card.

#### 2. DRAW PORTENT CARD

- **Keep Waking symbols visible**
- **Resolve special effects**
- **Summon foes at Rifts** (if available)

#### 3. INVESTIGATE OR FIGHT!

- **If in a Warded space**, draw a Hoard card.
- **If there are foes in sworn’s space**, they all attack you (roll dice shown on their card).

#### 4. RESOLVE END OF TURN

1. **End of Turn Effects**
2. **Fire!** - Roll 1 [STANDARD-DIE] per [FIRE] on sworn, then discard them.
3. **Check the Portent Discard Pile** - If there are 3 [SUMMONING], the Sleeper advances. Apply Sleeper and Vault card effects. Then, reshuffle Portent deck.
4. **Check Waking** - If a red space is reached or the working is disrupted, move the Stage 1 card to the side and resolve the Stage 2 Reveal effects, summoning the Sleeper
5. **Sleeper End of Turn Effects** - Resolve all revealed Stages in order.

### DICE AND CHECKS

**“Make a roll”** - Roll **3** [STANDARD-DIE] + any applicable [BONUS-DIE]

**Rerolls** - On ANY roll, the active sworn may take 1 sap to reroll 1 die (as many times as they want and can).

[SUCCESS] **Success** - Deals 1 wound when attacking.

[ELDER-SIGN] **Glyph** - Requires an ability to have any effect.

[TENTACLE] **Blight** - Lose 1 marrow.

### MARROW

**When marker reaches [INSANITY-THRESHOLD]:**

- Ignore any remaining marrow loss.
- Activate your bloom.
- Level up 1 of your skills.
- If there’s a [BONUS-DIE] symbol, sworn now adds +1 [BONUS-DIE] to all their rolls.

### FIGHTING THE SLEEPER

- Once summoned, if the Sleeper attacks, roll the dice on all revealed Stages together.
- The Sleeper can only be attacked after the working is disrupted.
- Once the current Sleeper Stage loses all its health, move that card to the side and resolve the Reveal effect of the next Stage.

### ENDING THE GAME

**Sworn all win if:**

- They defeat the Final Stage of the Sleeper.

**Sworn all lose if:**

- A sworn is eliminated before the Sleeper is summoned.
- All sworn are eliminated.
- The Rousing token reaches the final Waking track space.
