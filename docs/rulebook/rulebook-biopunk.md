## OVERVIEW

At the start of each game, players choose a Job box and a Growth box and combine their components. The Job will provide the map layout, the batch they are trying to disrupt (and how to disrupt it), as well as the abilities of the byproducts in the game. The Growth will provide the big boss they will fight, with its own challenges, along with its special minions. **Do not** combine elements from other boxes.

1 to 5 players control sinkers, cooperating to win or lose the game together. In each game, the goal remains the same: disrupt the batch, then kill the Growth.

## PLANT BOARD

The Plant board, used in every game, is the hub of information.

The Batch track shows the progression of the Growth as it manifests into our world. The Growth figure starts on the left part of the track and progresses to the right. When it enters a red space, it will be summoned (it will be summoned earlier if the Batch is disrupted). After the Growth is summoned to our world, the Escalation token is placed on the track and moved instead of the figure. If the Escalation token reaches the end of the track, the Growth dominates the world and the sinkers lose!

## SALVAGE CARDS

Each job has 15 Salvage cards. These include Items, Strays, and Afflictions that can help or hinder the players. Most Salvage cards can be claimed by fulfilling the requirements listed in the card’s central section. Sinkers claim either the left or right side of the card, tucking them under their sinker board on the appropriate side. Salvage cards are further explained on page 16.

If it’s not specified when the player may use an effect on a Salvage card, it may be used anytime during their turn. However, it cannot be used while resolving another card, effect, or during a roll.

## INCIDENT CARDS

Incident cards drive the horrors of each game. Players will grow to hate them. Half the Incident cards in a game come from the Job box and the other half comes from the Growth box.

Each turn, after taking their actions, the active player will draw an Incident card. Some will move and/or summon hostiles. Others will drive sinkers to slip. There are also ones that aren’t as kind. They are explained in detail on page 15.

[SUMMONING] Most Incident cards have a Batch Tick symbol on them. When there are 3 Batch Tick symbols in the Incident discard pile, the Growth advances along the Batch track (see page 18).

## HOSTILES

The term “hostiles” refers to Cooks, byproducts, and the Growth (once it’s summoned to the board). Any hostile that’s not a Cook or the Growth is a “byproduct”. Anything that refers to Cooks affects Cooks only. Anything that refers to byproducts affects byproducts only. Anything that refers to the Growth affects the Growth only.

Hostile cards show their health, the dice they use when attacking, and a special ability that may come into effect in different circumstances. The cards also show the total number of figures that should be available in the pool. If a Job uses specific byproducts, all its miniatures that are not present in the setup are kept in a pool, ready to spawn.

## SINKERS

Each sinker has a board with a wound track, a strain track, a grip track, and 3 skills. All are tracked with plastic rot markers in the sinker’s color. There are also slots on the right and on the left of the board to insert claimed Salvage cards. There is no limit to the number of cards that can be slotted to a sinker.

### STRAIN

Strain is used to try and push situations in the sinker’s favor. Players may always increase sinker’s strain to reroll 1 die they just rolled. Many Salvage cards also have sinkers take strain in order to claim them. Generally, the only consequence of being at maximum strain is that the sinker can’t take more strain (though some effects may deal wounds instead). Sinkers can take a maximum of 4 strain without healing.

### WOUNDS

If the marker ever reaches the skull at the end of the wound track, the sinker is dead (see Death of a Sinker on page 19).

### SKILLS

The sinker’s 3 skills allow them to bend the rules in their favor. The top one is their signature skill, unique to each sinker, while the other 2 skills come from a common pool (so other sinkers may have the same ones). All skills start at the first level, but sinkers will increase them when they level up. Some skills replace a lower-level skill as they level up, while others add to them, as described on the skill. The description of each of the common skills can be found on page 22.

### GRIP

Whenever a sinker loses grip, the marker on their grip track advances to the right. If it reaches the skull at the end, the sinker is lost to the slip and eliminated (see Ending the Game on page 20).

The track has Slip ([INSANITY-THRESHOLD]) spaces that will activate the sinker’s slip and level up a skill. Some of them also give sinkers permanent Kick dice. When a sinker loses more grip (from dice rolls, Salvage, or Incident cards) than required to hit the next [INSANITY-THRESHOLD], the marker stops at the [INSANITY-THRESHOLD] and all excess grip loss is ignored.

#### - Gaining Kick Dice

4 of the [INSANITY-THRESHOLD] spaces display a green Kick die [BONUS-DIE] under them. Once each of these is reached, the sinker adds +1 Kick die to any rolls they make for the rest of the  game, except for rolls against themselves (see page 11).

#### - Activating Slips

Each sinker gets a slip card at the start of play. Every time their marker on the grip track reaches a [INSANITY-THRESHOLD], that slip activates. Perform the symptoms on the card. If multiple sinkers reach a [INSANITY-THRESHOLD] at the same time, they activate clockwise from the current player.

#### - Leveling up Skills

When the marker reaches a [INSANITY-THRESHOLD], after activating their slip, the sinker levels up 1 of their skills. Choose 1 skill and move its marker 1 space to the right

## DICE AND CHECKS

Throughout the game, players roll dice to determine the outcome of certain actions or effects. The most common times to roll dice are when players are told to make a roll and when a hostile attacks.

### Make a Roll

Some actions and Salvage cards tell players to "make a roll". When players make a roll, they always roll 3 standard black dice (and may also be allowed to add kick green dice).

### Hostile Attack

Hostiles roll a specific number and type of dice for their attacks as indicated on their reference card.

### Rolls Against Yourself

Some effects and abilities specify "rolls against yourself". Rolls against a player are rolls where each success is detrimental to the player. Hostile attacks and rolling for Fire (see page 17) are examples of "rolls against yourself".

Each die has 4 different possible results:

- [SUCCESS] **Success:** This means that the sinker (or the hostile) succeeded at their attempt (or partially succeeded). If the sinker was attacking, it means they hit. If a hostile is attacking, it means they hit. In some cases, players need to reach a target amount of successes in a single roll.

- [ELDER-SIGN] **Grit**: These mean nothing unless a sinker has a skill or card that uses them.

- [TENTACLE] **Rot:** The Slip! For each rot, the sinker loses 1 grip, moving their tracker 1 space to the right.

> **IMPORTANT:** Rots on EVERY roll cost sinkers grip, whether they're attacking, being attacked, or just "making a roll".

- [BLANK] **Blank:** No effect most of the time.

### KICK DICE

Certain skills and cards may give sinkers kick green dice, as noted on their description. Some checks may also give them kick dice if they have a related ability. Finally, reaching certain Slips will add permanent kick dice to all of that sinker's rolls, except against themself.

> **NOTE:** The kick dice do not contain a rot icon.

> **NOTE:** There is no limit to the number of regular dice and kick dice that may be added to a roll (if players run out of dice, note the results and roll the same dice again).

> **NOTE:** When rolling dice, players cannot choose to roll less dice than they are allowed to roll (unless the effect is optional).

### REROLLS

After rolling dice, players may take 1 strain to reroll 1 die, ignoring its original result. Players may do this as often as they like on any roll they make, until the sinker's strain is at the maximum. When hostiles attack, players roll for them and may also take strain to reroll their dice.

### MODIFYING ROLLS

Some effects or abilities may be applied to rolls. Unless stated otherwise, those abilities are applied to ALL rolls a sinker makes, including against themselves. Note, though, that some of them are optional.

### APPLYING RESULTS

Once a player is done using any rerolls, tally the number of each result obtained (note that some die faces contain 2 results). Make sure to apply any symbol changes from abilities (such as counting [ELDER-SIGN] as successes).

> **NOTE:** If an effect counts an icon as another icon (e.g. Chem Sense), that icon stops counting as the original icon, unless stated otherwise in the effect. That means that an icon cannot be changed by 2 different abilities, unless one of those abilities says the icon keeps counting as the original icon.

Then, apply the results of the dice roll in this order:

#### 1. Use Any Successes:

- If a sinker is attacking, these successes are wounds applied to their target.
- If a hostile is attacking, successes are also wounds applied to their target (usually a sinker).
- If players are told to "make a roll" by an action or effect, they must follow the instructions on the corresponding action or effect. Sometimes, they must match or exceed the number of successes indicated on the card to be successful. In other cases, for each success rolled, players get to do something.

> **[Figure]** This job allows the sinker to make a special action. While they are in a space without Cooks, make a roll. Then, they move the nearest Cook 1 space towards them for each success rolled.

#### 2. Resolve Effects:

- Resolve any effects that may be tied to the result of the roll.

*(Examples: "If wounded...", "If it survives an attack...", "When the hostile dies...")*

#### 3. Apply Any Rots:

- Lose 1 grip for each rot rolled (move the marker 1 space to the right on their grip track).

- If the sinker reaches a [INSANITY-THRESHOLD] space on their grip track, stop moving the marker (even if they didn't lose the full amount of grip), activate their slip, and level up a skill (see Grip on page 8).

## 1. TAKE 3 ACTIONS

On a player's turn, they may take 3 actions. They may take the same action more than once. There are 4 actions that are available in every job and 2 that are unique to each job. Also, some effects may give players access to free and special actions.

Some actions can only be done while in Clear Air. Clear Air is a space without hostiles.

### FREE ACTIONS

Some abilities and effects can be used as a free action. They can only be used during this Phase of the turn before or after other actions.

### SPECIAL ACTIONS

Some abilities and effects can be used as a special action. They can only be used during this Phase of the turn and players must spend 1 of their actions to use it.

### RUN

Move up to 3 spaces. Sinkers can only move between spaces if there is a passage on both tiles (as indicated by arrows on both sides). They may also pass between 2 spaces that both have a staircase or tunnel token.

When sinkers leave a space with hostiles, ALL hostiles in that space follow them to their new space (even if there were other sinkers in their space). When sinkers leave a space with Fire tokens, place 1 Fire token from the reserve onto their sinker board for each token on the space, leaving those tokens untouched (see Fire! on page17).

> **NOTE:** *Unless stated otherwise, when a sinker moves outside of a Run action, they catch fire and hostiles follow as well, even if it's not their turn. The only exception are the effects that "place" a sinker in another space, in which case the sinker is placed directly in that space, they do not catch fire, and hostiles do not follow.*

> **[Example]** *EXAMPLE: 1- Bram uses a Run action to move up to 3 spaces. He first moves into a space with a Cook that has a wound.*

> **[Example]** *2- He keeps moving and the Cook follows him to the new space. The second space has 2 Fire tokens, but no hostiles.*

> **[Example]** *3- Bram finishes his Run by moving into a third space, which contains an Angler. Again, the Cook follows him. He is also leaving a space with 2 Fire tokens, so he takes 2 Fire tokens from the reserve and adds them to his sinker board. He is now in a space with an Angler and the Cook who followed him. He is also on Fire. Pretty typical evening.*

### ATTACK

Target a single hostile in the sinker's space and make a roll (see page 11). Each success rolled causes 1 wound to the target. Place wound tokens next to the base of the figure attacked. If this number equals (or exceeds) their health, they die. Remove their figure from the board. Keep in mind that if the Growth is on the board, it can only be attacked once the batch is disrupted (see page 19).

> **[Example]** *EXAMPLE: 1- Bram uses an action to attack a Cook and an Angler. Normally, he would have to target either the Cook or the Angler, but his level 2 Brawling skill enables him to target all hostiles in his space, so he doesn't have to choose.*

> **[Example]** *2- He takes 3 standard dice (players always take 3 standard dice when making a roll) and 1 kick die for his Brawling skill.*

> **[Example]** *3- He rolls and gets a grit, a blank, a success + rot, and rot. Bram doesn't have a skill that utilizes the grit, so it has no effect. Bram takes a strain to reroll the grit, since it would make him lose a strain anyway because of the Angler's special ability and gets a blank. Bram's signature skill, Proficient, allows him to reroll an additional die. He could reroll the same die again, but chooses to reroll the blank result on the standard die and gets a success! He decides to stop rerolling.*

> **[Example]** *4- The Cook can take 2 wounds, and has already taken 1, and the Angler can take 3. Bram has 2 successes to distribute as hits. He decides to use 1 to kill the Cook, removing the figure and, using his Brawling skill, applies the last success as a hit to the Angler.*

> **NOTE:** *If the sinker didn't have the Brawling skill, he would have to deal all wounds to a single hostile (chosen before rolling dice), even if excess wounds would go ignored with no effect.*

> **[Example]** *5- Finally, Bram loses 2 Grip from the rots rolled. 2 steps closer to the slip.*

### BREATHE (only on Clear Air)

If a sinker is in Clear Air (there are no hostiles with them) they may heal their strain and/or health up to 3 spaces (in any combination). This is 3 spaces total, not 3 spaces for each track. Sinkers may perform the Breathe action more than once per turn.

> **IMPORTANT:** **GRIP CANNOT BE HEALED BY BREATHING!**

> **[Example]** *EXAMPLE: Odile is at full strain and has taken 1 wound. She spends an action to Breathe. She could choose to heal 1 wound and 2 strain, but decides to heal 3 strain instead, leaving her health where it is.*

### TRADE

The active sinker and all sinkers in their space may trade any number of Items or Strays with each other (either giving or receiving). While some Salvage cards have Items or Strays on both the left and right side of the card, a traded card must remain on the same side as it was originally. **Sinkers may not trade Afflictions**.

## 2. DRAW INCIDENT CARD

Draw the top Incident card from the deck and resolve it. Then, place it on a faceup discard pile.

Incident cards may have several different steps to them, divided into 3 types. Players must do these steps in order, from top to bottom, before discarding the card. If players can't do a step on the card, skip it and move on to the next.

### BATCH TICK

The Batch Tick symbol has no effect when the card is drawn, but may cause the Growth to advance on its track at the end of the turn (see page 18). If the card has this symbol, make sure it remains visible when it is discarded.

### SPECIAL EFFECTS

Incident cards have all sorts of different special effects to make life hell. Unless otherwise stated, these effects only affect the player whose turn it is, ignoring all other sinkers. Any choice that needs to be made is up to the active player, such as choosing which closest figure to move.

When an effect moves a hostile towards a space, it should be moved by the shortest path to that space. Count how many spaces the hostile would move in all possible paths and choose the shortest one. In case of a tie, the active player chooses. The hostile must always be moved as much as allowed towards its target but stops when it reaches its target.

### SUMMON HOSTILES

Finally, the bottom of some cards will indicate a Drain color and the specific hostile that must be summoned there. If players have the corresponding figure available in reserve, place it in the indicated space. Otherwise, ignore this step. If there are multiple Drains and hostiles indicated, perform the summonings in order. Skip any remaining Drains if there are no more hostile figures available to summon.

## 3. INVESTIGATE OR FIGHT!

This phase varies depending on whether there are hostiles in a sinker's space or not.

**If the sinkers is in Clear Air, INVESTIGATE the space:** Draw the top card of the Salvage deck and read the text in the center box aloud.

Some cards result in CLAIMING a Stray, Item, or Affliction. Claiming means to slide the card under the sinker board, becoming part of the sinker's inventory (or, when it's an Affliction, changing their mental state). If the sinker cannot or, in case it's optional, does not want to claim either side of the card, discard the card.

If the sinker doesn't have that skill, they gain level 1 in that skill while they have this Stray with them (take the corresponding token). > **NOTE:** *Sinkers must always investigate if they are safe, even if they wouldn't want to.*

**If you are not in Clear Air, the hostiles FIGHT you:**
Do not draw a Salvage card. Instead, each hostile in their space attacks them in the order the player chooses (they ignore other sinkers) until all hostiles in the space that could attack you have done so.

If an effect makes the space safe during this phase, hostiles stop attacking (but the sinker cannot investigate, since the space wasn't safe at the start of the phase).

## 4. RESOLVE END OF TURN

### 1. END OF TURN EFFECTS

Any effects that happen at the end of the turn, except for Growth effects, happen now, in any order the active player chooses.

### 2. FIRE!

Sinkers may be on fire. This will have happened if they left a space that contains Fire tokens. It may be that a byproduct set them on Fire. At this point, it really doesn't matter, does it?

[FIRE] At the end of a player's turn, they roll 1 standard die for every Fire token on their sinker board, as a single roll. You may use strain to reroll dice. Take 1 wound for each success and lose 1 grip for each rot.

Then, they discard all Fire tokens on their sinker board.

> **NOTE:** *Hostiles don't catch Fire when leaving a space with Fire tokens.*

> **RUNNING OUT OF FIRE:** *The number of Fire tokens on the board is limited to the number of Fire tokens available. If players need to add a Fire token to the board but there are none left, ignore the effect. However, Fire tokens on sinkers are not limited. If a player needs to add a Fire token to their sinker board but there are none left, use a wound token instead. If there are Fire tokens available on Sinker boards, replace them with wound tokens to make the Fire tokens available to place on the board.*

## DEATH OF A SINKER

When a sinker is killed or lost to the slip, the game is immediately lost if the Growth is still on the Batch track. If, however, the Growth has already been summoned to the board, the other sinkers may continue playing and trying to win the game. First, discard all Salvage cards, tokens and other components the now-deceased sinker had. The job may specify something to do with any special tokens the sinker had been holding.

If the sinker died during their turn, skip all the following turn phases except for the Check the Incident Discard Pile phase. If there are 3 Batch Tick symbols, the Growth advances and its effects are applied (if it is needed to determine the position of the dead sinker, use the space where they died). From now on, that player's turn is skipped entirely.

## DISRUPTING THE BATCH

Each job describes the tasks the sinkers must accomplish in order to disrupt the Cooks' batch and make the Growth mortal, even if only for a short time. The Growth cannot be attacked or damaged until the batch is disrupted (though it can attack sinkers if it's on the board)!

If the sinkers disrupt the Batch, the Growth is summoned at the end of the turn (see Check the Batch Tick on the previous page).

## FIGHTING THE GROWTH

Once the Growth is summoned to the board, it acts like any hostile, attacking sinkers that end their turn in its space. The number and type of dice the Growth rolls is the total of all dice displayed on all its revealed Stage cards.

Until the Batch is disrupted, the Growth cannot be attacked or damaged, even if it's already been summoned to the board by reaching the red space on the batch track! After the Batch is disrupted, the Growth can be attacked and damaged like any other hostile. The Growth has multiple Stages, with individual health totals, which must be defeated one at a time until it's ultimately destroyed.

**Starting with Stage 2, each card displays**:
- A one-time effect that takes place when that card is revealed.
- An ongoing effect that remains in play until the end of the game.
- Health showing how many wounds that Stage can take.
- The number and type of dice that Stage adds to the Growth's attacks.

When a Stage has wounds equal to or greater than its health, that Stage is defeated. Move that Stage card to the side to reveal the next and resolve its When Revealed effect. Any excess wounds do NOT carry over to the next Stage. Remember that all of the previous Stages' effects and dice remain in play until the end of the game!

> **[Example]** *EXAMPLE: A sinker attacks THE MAW while it is in Stage 2. That Stage already has 9 wounds from earlier attacks. The attacking sinker does 5 wounds! This new total of 14 is enough to defeat this Stage's 12 health. The Stage card is moved to the side, next to Stage 1, revealing Stage 3. Even though the sinker only needed 12 wounds to defeat the Stage and did 14, the extra 2 wounds do NOT carry over to Stage 3. THE MAW is moved to the Yellow Drain and each sinker gains 1 Fatigue token unless they take 2 strain. THE MAW now rolls 3 kick dice AND 2 standard dice when attacking!*

## COMPONENT LIMITATION

With the exception of dice and Wound tokens (and consequently, Fire on sinkers), all components are limited. If there are not enough tokens (or other components), use as many as possible for the effect, but do not add substitutes for the missing components. Also, if a component is discarded/removed, it returns to the pool of available components, unless stated otherwise.

When mixing components from different *The Sump* Core Boxes, players should increase the Fire token pool. For example, since *The Sump Core Box* comes with 8 Fire tokens and *The Sump - The Deep* Core Box with 12, if players have both boxes, they increase the pool to 20 Fire tokens.

Note that this is not true for other components. For example, the total number of Cooks in a Job is always limited to 10, as stated in the Growth Minions Card.

## RESOLVING TIES AND AMBIGUITIES

If there are multiple ways to resolve an effect, the active player always chooses how to resolve it. For example, if an effect moves the nearest hostile and there are multiple hostiles at the same distance, the active player chooses 1 of them to move. Also, if multiple effects trigger at the same time, the active player chooses the order in which they are resolved.

For several effects, players must determine the nearest or furthest element (space, figure, or token) from their sinker or another element on the board. When determining the nearest or furthest element, always use the shortest path to the space where the element is. Distance to the same space is always 0. So, if an element is in the sinker's space, it is the nearest element. If there are multiple elements of the specified type at the same distance, the active player selects 1 of them.

## CHECKING DISTANCE

For several effects players must determine the nearest or furthest element (space, figure or token) from their sinker or another element on the board. When determining the nearest or furthest element, always use the shortest path to the space where the element is. Distance to the same space is always 0. So, if an element is in the sinker's space, it is the nearest element. If there are multiple elements of the specified type at the same distance, the active player selects 1 of them.

Effects that block passages, like locked tokens, should be considered when checking the shortest path, the path cannot pass through blocked passages. The only exception is if the effect would move a figure that can ignore the restrictions. For example, on Season 3 - Job 1: Isle of the Damned, the Cooks can pass through locked tokens. If an effect moves the nearest Cook towards you, even though other figures cannot move through the locked tokens, you ignore those tokens when checking which Cook is the nearest, since they can pass through them. However, effects that affect which spaces are adjacent to each other on specific circumstances, like the Silver Key Rig, are ignored when checking the shortest path.

## ENDING THE GAME

**Players win the game** when they kill the Growth by defeating its Final Stage. If multiple end-game triggers happen at the same time, winning conditions have precedence.

> **[Example]** *EXAMPLE: The Growth and the last sinker die at the same time. The players saved the world and win the game.*

**Players lose the game** when 1 of the following happens:
- A sinker is killed or lost to the slip before the Growth is summoned to the board (Stage 1 card).
- All sinkers are killed or lost to the slip after the Growth is summoned (Stage 2 onward).
- The Escalation token reaches the last (8th) space of the Batch track.

If players want to adjust the difficulty of the game, they can implement some of the following changes.

**-Breaking the Deep Rules**

[GATE-BLUE] [GATE-RED] [GATE-YELLOW] [LOC-START]

> **[Figure]** [SKILL-STEALTH] STEALTH

> - Deal 1 wound to each hostile you Sneak past.

Stealth specifies that the sinker may only Sneak during a Run action. If any other effect makes them move, hostiles follow as usual, unless stated otherwise.

With Stealth Level 4, the sinker keeps Level 3’s ability to deal 1 wound to each hostile they Sneak past.

## RULES SUMMARY

### TURN SEQUENCE

#### 1. SINKER TAKES 3 ACTIONS

- **Run** - Move up to 3 spaces - Hostiles follow. Add 1 [FIRE] to the sinker board per [FIRE] in the space they leave.
- **Attack** - Make a roll against 1 hostile in sinker’s space
- **Breathe** - Clear Air only: Heal 3 strain/wounds..
- **Trade** - With all sinkers in same space.
- **Job actions** - Described on the job card.

#### 2. DRAW INCIDENT CARD

- **Keep Batch Tick symbols visible**
- **Resolve special effects**
- **Summon hostiles at Drains** (if available)

#### 3. INVESTIGATE OR FIGHT!

- **If in Clear Air**, draw a Salvage card.
- **If there are hostiles in sinker’s space**, they all attack you (roll dice shown on their card).

#### 4. RESOLVE END OF TURN

1. **End of Turn Effects**
2. **Fire!** - Roll 1 [STANDARD-DIE] per [FIRE] on sinker, then discard them.
3. **Check the Incident Discard Pile** - If there are 3 [SUMMONING], the Growth advances. Apply Growth and Job card effects. Then, reshuffle Incident deck.
4. **Check Batch Tick** - If a red space is reached or the Batch is disrupted, move the Stage 1 card to the side and resolve the Stage 2 Reveal effects, summoning the Growth
5. **Growth End of Turn Effects** - Resolve all revealed Stages in order.

### DICE AND CHECKS

**“Make a roll”** - Roll **3** [STANDARD-DIE] + any applicable [BONUS-DIE]

**Rerolls** - On ANY roll, the active sinker may take 1 strain to reroll 1 die (as many times as they want and can).

[SUCCESS] **Success** - Deals 1 wound when attacking.

[ELDER-SIGN] **Grit** - Requires an ability to have any effect.

[TENTACLE] **Rot** - Lose 1 grip.

### GRIP

**When marker reaches [INSANITY-THRESHOLD]:**

- Ignore any remaining grip loss.
- Activate your slip.
- Level up 1 of your skills.
- If there’s a [BONUS-DIE] symbol, sinker now adds +1 [BONUS-DIE] to all their rolls.

### FIGHTING THE GROWTH

- Once summoned, if the Growth attacks, roll the dice on all revealed Stages together.
- The Growth can only be attacked after the batch is disrupted.
- Once the current Growth Stage loses all its health, move that card to the side and resolve the Reveal effect of the next Stage.

### ENDING THE GAME

**Sinkers all win if:**

- They defeat the Final Stage of the Growth.

**Sinkers all lose if:**

- A sinker is eliminated before the Growth is summoned.
- All sinkers are eliminated.
- The Escalation token reaches the final Batch track space.
